In the parish of Conxo, south of Compostela, there is a hill where the morning mist lingers longer than anywhere else. Among the leaves, the stones of a castle still peek through. There are no towers or battlements: only a few pieces of wall and the outline of the foundations, patient, as if someone had drawn on the ground the memory of a building. It was called the Rocha Forte. Today the wind passes over it without finding anything to strike. And now, get comfortable. Turn off the light, if it is still on. Let your body grow a little heavier on the mattress, and let your breathing slow down. You don't have to remember anything I tell you. It is enough to let yourself drift. Let's go back, to the Galicia of more than five centuries ago.
